# Chapter 9: PLotting and Visualization

Making information visualization (sometimes called _plots_) is one of the most
important tasks in data analysis. It may be a part of the exploratory process --
for example, to help identify outliers or needed data transformations, or as a
way of generating ideas for models. For others, building an interactive
visualization for the web may be the end goal. Python has many add-on libraries
for making static or dynamic visualizations, but I'll be mainly focused on
matplotlibs and libraries that build on top of that.
